# MANAAR TRAVELS & TOURS - WORKSPACE GUIDELINES

## Islamic & Modest Travel Aesthetic Guidelines

1. **Islamic & Muslim Values First**:
   - The platform is owned by a Muslim business (**Manaar Travels & Tours Limited**).
   - All visual assets, imagery, copy, and human depictions must strictly adhere to Islamic values and modest standards.
   - Any imagery featuring people must portray modest attire (e.g., pilgrims in Ihram, modest traditional thobes, jalabiyas, hijabs, abayas, or respectful professional dress).
   - Avoid any immodest, inappropriate, or non-halal imagery (e.g., alcohol, non-modest swimwear, Gambling, non-halal cuisine).

2. **Pilgrimage & Sacred Travel Integrity**:
   - Prioritize authentic, high-quality architectural imagery of Masjid al-Haram, Al-Masjid an-Nabawi, Makkah Mukarramah, Madinah Munawwarah, Islamic heritage sites, and premium halal family hospitality.
   - All reviews, customer profiles, and staff accounts must maintain respectful Islamic titles (e.g., Alhaji, Hajiya, Imam, Scholar) and dignified phrasing ("Assalamu Alaikum", "JazakAllahu Khairan", "May Allah bless your pilgrimage").
